01 · At the interface

Total internal reflection

A ray inside the core meets the core–cladding boundary at angle of incidence θᵢ, measured from the normal. Below the critical angle it splits into a reflected and a refracted ray; at or beyond it, every photon reflects.

02 · Inside the fibre

Numerical aperture & the acceptance cone

A ray entering the end face at angle θ₀ refracts into the core, then strikes the wall at angle φ from the wall's normal. Only rays launched inside the acceptance cone keep φ beyond the critical angle at every bounce.
